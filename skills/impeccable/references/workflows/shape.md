# Shape Workflow

Discover what should be made and how it should work, then return a confirmed design brief without code.

## Discover

Ask two or three questions in one round, adding a second round only for a material gap:

- What job must this surface complete, for whom, and in what situation?
- What is the primary action or understanding, and what proves success?
- What real content, data, assets, states, ranges, and constraints must it carry?
- What must remain untouched, and what would make a polished result wrong?

Do not ask for implementation values. Reuse facts already established in `PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md`, and the repository.

## Resolve

Choose the visitor mode and establish visual authority: existing system, incomplete system, approved replacement, or no visual authority. For a new or replacement world, follow the direction selection in `new-work.md` but stop before implementation.

## Return the brief

Include:

1. Job, audience, mode, and primary outcome.
2. Real proof, content, data ranges, and material states.
3. Selected direction, hierarchy, interaction thesis, and focal moment.
4. Scope, boundaries, anti-goals, and untouched areas.
5. Responsive, accessibility, performance, localization, and platform constraints.
6. Open decisions the implementer must not invent.

Ask for one confirmation or correction round, then stop without writing code.
