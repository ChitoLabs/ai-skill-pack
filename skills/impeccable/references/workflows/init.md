# Product Context Workflow

Capture durable product truth in `PRODUCT.md`. Do not choose a visual world or write `DESIGN.md` here.

## Inspect

Read existing product docs, routes, features, roles, copy, assets, legal claims, platform signals, accessibility commitments, and any existing `PRODUCT.md`. Repository evidence is a hypothesis until the user confirms it.

## Ask

Ask only about material gaps that current evidence does not answer:

1. Who is the primary user, in what situation, and what job are they doing?
2. What does the product make possible, and what mechanism or position is meaningfully different?
3. Which constraints, assets, facts, terms, evidence, and accessibility needs must future work preserve?

Use at most three focused questions per round. Require one answer or explicit approval before creating a new file. Mark unresolved facts instead of inventing them. Do not ask for colors, typography, or style.

## Write

Create or update `PRODUCT.md` with only durable, confirmed content:

- Platform: `web`, `ios`, `android`, or `adaptive`
- Users and jobs
- Product purpose and positioning
- Operating context
- Capabilities and constraints
- Brand commitments
- Evidence on hand
- Three to five product principles
- Accessibility and inclusion requirements
- Open decisions, clearly labeled

If `PRODUCT.md` exists, present the proposed merge or replacement scope before overwriting confirmed content. Preserve `DESIGN.md` unchanged.

## Return

Report facts captured, inferences confirmed, unresolved decisions, the file path, and the supported next workflow that matches the user's request.
